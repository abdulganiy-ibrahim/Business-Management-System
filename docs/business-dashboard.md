# Business Dashboard V1 Pages

## 1. Dashboard

**Responsibility:**
Display a clear overview of the business and its current performance.

**Contains:**

* Business overview
* Key business statistics
* Sales overview
* Recent sales/activity
* Top-selling products
* Basic inventory/low-stock information
* Collapsible AI chatbot

---

## 2. Products

**Responsibility:**
Display and manage the products belonging to the selected business.

**Contains:**

* Product list
* Add product UI
* Product details
* Most-selling products

---

## 3. Sales

**Responsibility:**
Manage and display sales made by the selected business.

**Contains:**

* Sales list
* Add/record sale UI
* Sale details
* Sale items

---

## 4. Inventory

**Responsibility:**
Display the inventory history of the selected business.

**Contains:**

* Inventory history
* Stock additions
* Stock reductions
* Sale-related inventory changes

---

## 5. AI Assistant

**Responsibility:**
Provide a dedicated interface for interacting with the business AI assistant.

**Contains:**

* Chat UI
* Conversation history
* Suggested prompts
* Business-related questions and insights
